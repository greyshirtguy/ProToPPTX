ProToPPTX for the Mac
An early build. It needs macOS 15 or later.


WHAT IT IS

It turns ProPresenter presentations (.pro and .probundle files) into
PowerPoint files that look the same, slide for slide, with the words as text
boxes you can edit, not pictures of slides. Media cues go over too, and the
background a slide's media cue puts up is kept on the slides after it, as
ProPresenter shows them (an option: on as it comes).

It only reads your presentations. It never changes them, or anything in
ProPresenter's folders.

It is one person's hobby. Nobody supports it and nothing is promised. It is not
ProPresenter and has nothing to do with Renewed Vision, who make ProPresenter,
or with Microsoft, who make PowerPoint.


HOW TO USE IT

Drop presentations, or folders of them, on its window (or use Add). Each one's
slides are shown as they will be in PowerPoint; the options are on the right,
with anything that could not be carried over. Export saves the one chosen;
Export All saves every one in the list into a folder you choose.

A .probundle has its pictures and videos in it. A .pro on its own has not: the
app looks for them where the presentation says they were, beside the file, and
in ProPresenter's own media folders on this Mac, and says which it could not
find.

Fonts are named in the PowerPoint file as in ProPresenter: a font that is not
installed on the Mac that shows the file is shown in another until it is.


OPENING IT THE FIRST TIME

This build is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, and Apple has
not checked it. So macOS will not open it at a double-click. To open it:

1. Drag ProToPPTX to Applications, and double-click it there.
   macOS says it could not verify the app. Close that message.
2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and scroll down to
   Security. It says ProToPPTX was blocked. Click Open Anyway, and say yes
   when it asks again.

After that it opens like any other app.

Do this only for something whose maker you have reason to trust. What this
app is made from can be read at
https://github.com/greyshirtguy/ProToPPTX
